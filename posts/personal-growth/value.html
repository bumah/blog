Value isn't just what you're worth on paper. In any room, any relationship, there's a way you carry yourself that quietly raises or lowers how much you're valued. Over time I've boiled it down to one word: VALUE. Here's what each letter means.

V — Validation

Don't hand out praise freely. When you approve of everything, your approval stops meaning anything. Give it only when it's genuinely earned, and it becomes something people actually want. Cheap praise is worth exactly what it costs.

A — Attention

Be a little scarce with it. Attention that's always available gets taken for granted. When you don't give all of yourself away at once, the attention you do give carries weight. Presence is more powerful when it isn't guaranteed.

L — Looks

Look after yourself — presentable, healthy, put-together. Whether we like it or not, we're visual creatures and looks matter. And it runs deeper than appearance: people tend to treat you the way you treat yourself. Take care of how you look and you signal that you're worth taking care of.

U — Unpredictable

Everything gets boring when it's too predictable. A bit of variety, a bit of mystery, keeps things alive. You don't have to be chaotic — just don't be so easy to read that there's nothing left to discover.

E — Eager

Don't be too eager. Keep some self-control. Wanting something too visibly, too fast, lowers your standing — it reads as need. Hold a little back, stay measured, and you keep the upper hand.

The key takeaway

Next time you're in any environment — work, a date, networking — run a quick check on yourself: am I doing anything that's hurting my value right now? Go through the five. Am I over-praising? Giving away too much attention? Letting my appearance slip? Being completely predictable? Coming across too eager?

You won't get all five right every time, and that's fine. The point is the awareness — catching the one or two things pulling your value down in the moment, so you can quietly adjust before they cost you.
