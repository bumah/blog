A balanced life is one of those phrases that sounds nice and means nothing until you get specific. I've spent a lot of time thinking about this. And I was very deliberate about not coming up with something society has conditioned us to think matters, but something personal. I also wanted something I could easily plan, schedule and measure, so I know whether I'm on track building the LIFE I want or not. After soul-searching, it came down to four things, and they happen to spell the word itself, LIFE, so it is easy for me to remember: Love, Interests, Fun, Exploration.

Love

Everything starts with love. And this is not love as in romance and all that, but self-love. Actually looking after myself. Body, health, mind, emotions, and the way I treat myself day to day. Personally, this has to come first, because people usually don't get the best version of you when you're run down. Everything else sits on top of this one.

Interests

The things that genuinely pull you in. The things you can do endlessly, hobbies and stuff you'd do whether or not anyone was watching or paying. I have a saying. "Your interests are what make you interesting." A life with no interests of its own is just work and obligation.

Fun

This is the easiest one to defer forever. There is always a reason to postpone it. Spending life entirely building toward some day that may never come is not much of a life. So I try not to defer fun; I schedule it.

Exploration

This is about staying curious. Reading, learning, meeting new people, seeing the world. There's always more out there than the small patch you already know and live in.

For me, at the end of each week I ask myself one question: how was my LIFE this week? Then I score each of the four out of 5. It takes only a few minutes. Did I look after myself? Did I pursue my interests? Did I have a bit of fun? Did I learn or experience something new? I have a diary to note the things I'm doing for each pillar so I can fall back on. A low score somewhere shows you where you fell short, so you can put a bit more in there the following week. Some weeks won't be balanced. That's fine. I'm not chasing a perfect scorecard, just trying to notice before one of the four quietly disappears for months.
