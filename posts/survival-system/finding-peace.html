There is a time in your youth when you are always after something. More money, more recognition, the next big opportunity or even an argument you will have won and forgotten by next week. But eventually one comes to the conclusion that a certain calmness of mind has more value.

I was putting some thought into what I can rely on to steady myself when I am under stress or simply carrying too much, be it from annoyance or disappointment. The word PEACE came to me.

I would not say I have any of it down to a science. Far from it. Still, they are the reminders I put forward.

P - Priorities

When there is enough on your plate, everything takes on a sense of urgency that it does not deserve. In other words, the more things you carry, the more chaos you invite. Most stress we have isn't from one big problem, but from too many small things all asking for attention at once. I make a point of asking what is of consequence and if it is not, I drop it.

E - Expectations

You can get frustrated by wanting life to dispense its rewards in some fair and orderly fashion, or by expecting others to act as you would. It is not how it works. Life doesn't owe you anything. Most disappointment is just the gap between what happened and what you assumed you were owed. I've realised that when I close that gap by expecting less, the less let down I find myself.

A - Acceptance

A plan goes awry, bad news arrives, someone fails to deliver. You may not like it, but once it is done, there is no use in battling it in your head. Acceptance isn't giving up. To me it's refusing to fight reality after it's already happened. The fighting is what steals the peace, not the event.

C - Control

You've probably heard this cliche a million times. "Focus on what you can control." This is the one I find most useful. Is there something I can do? If so, do it. Otherwise, what is the point in shouldering it all day? Easier said than done, but it is a good question to ask.

E - Ego

On occasion my ire is not over anything of substance but because I feel the need to show I am right, or that I have been disrespected. In truth, not every point is worth making and not every argument calls for a victory.

Peace is not having no problems. It is learning not to turn every problem into something you carry around with you.

That is what I am trying to get better at anyway.
