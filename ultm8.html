ULTM8

Add more life to your years.

ULTM8 is a free longevity tool that helps you build more vitality and freedom in your life, especially as you grow older.

Living longer matters. But so does having the health to enjoy those years, the independence to live them on your terms, and the financial resilience to keep your choices open.

ULTM8 focuses on eight areas across two foundations: Vitality and Freedom.

Vitality

The physical capacity to stay active, capable and independent.

Heart health

The cardiovascular foundation for a longer, healthier life.

Strength

The physical capacity to remain capable and independent as you age.

Stamina

The endurance to stay active, recover well and keep doing the things you enjoy.

Lifestyle

The everyday behaviours, including sleep, stress, nutrition and habits, that influence everything else.

Freedom

The financial resilience that keeps your choices open.

Debt

What you owe, what it costs you, and how much it limits your future choices.

Protection

The financial buffers and safeguards that prevent setbacks from becoming crises.

Retirement

Whether what you're building today can support the life you want later.

Income

The resources that fund your life today and create greater choice tomorrow.
