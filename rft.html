Risk-First Thinking

Stop failure before it stops you.

Risk-First Thinking improves your chances of success by anticipating risks and addressing them early, before they cost you. Most people ask what they should add to achieve their goal; RFT asks what roadblocks they should remove to achieve their goal.

The GPS method

GGoal

Define your goal and what could stop it.

PProtect

Rank threats and put controls.

SSteer

Take action and adapt continuously.

In practice

Goal: lose weight.

Traditional thinking

Eat healthy. Exercise more.

  • Buy healthy meals.
  • Plan to go to the gym regularly.
Risk-first thinking

Stop eating badly and missing workouts.

  • Remove all trigger foods from the house.
  • Prepare meals in advance.
  • Find an accountability partner.
  • Have a fallback home workout session.
